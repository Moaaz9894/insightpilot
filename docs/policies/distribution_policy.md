# Route-to-Market and Distribution Policy

## Outlet segmentation
Outlets are grouped into Supermarket, Convenience and Wholesale channels. Each channel has a defined visit frequency: supermarkets weekly, convenience stores twice weekly, wholesale accounts on demand with a minimum order size.

## Delivery windows
Standard deliveries are made within 48 hours of order confirmation. Urgent orders are delivered within 24 hours and carry an express fee unless the outlet is a strategic account.

## Route optimisation
Routes are reviewed monthly. A route is split when average daily drops exceed 35 outlets or when drive time exceeds 6 hours.
