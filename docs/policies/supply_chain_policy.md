# Supply Chain and Inventory Policy

## Safety stock
Safety stock is set to cover 7 days of forecast demand for fast-moving products and 14 days for products with unreliable supply. Safety stock levels are recalculated every month from the latest demand forecast.

## Reorder rules
A replenishment order is triggered when stock on hand falls below the safety stock plus expected demand during the supplier lead time. Orders above 50,000 units need supply planning manager approval.

## Expiry management
Products must leave the warehouse with at least 60 percent of their shelf life remaining. Stock under 30 percent of shelf life is reported to finance for markdown or write-off.
