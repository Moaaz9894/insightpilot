# Promotions and Discounts Policy

## Discount limits
Standard promotional discounts are capped at 15 percent of list price. Discounts between 15 and 25 percent need regional sales manager approval. Discounts above 25 percent are not permitted.

## Promotion duration
A promotion may run for at most 21 consecutive days. A cooling-off period of 14 days is required before the same product can be promoted again in the same outlet.

## Post-promotion review
Every promotion must be reviewed within 10 days of ending. The review compares incremental volume against the baseline forecast and calculates promotion return on investment. Promotions with negative return are not renewed.
