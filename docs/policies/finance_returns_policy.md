# Credit Terms and Returns Policy

## Credit terms
Standard payment terms are 30 days from invoice. New customers start with 14 days and a credit limit set by finance. Accounts overdue by more than 45 days are placed on hold until the balance is cleared.

## Returns
Returns are accepted only for damaged goods or products delivered with less than the agreed shelf life. Returns must be reported within 3 days of delivery with photographic evidence.

## Credit notes
Credit notes are issued within 10 working days of an approved return. Credit notes above 5,000 in value need finance manager sign-off.
