# Pricing Policy

## Price changes
List prices are reviewed quarterly. Any price change above 5 percent requires approval from the Revenue Growth Management lead and must be communicated to customers at least 14 days before it takes effect.

## Price floors
No product may be sold below its price floor, which is set at cost plus the minimum margin of 12 percent. Exceptions require written approval from the regional finance manager.

## Competitive matching
Sales representatives may match a documented competitor price for a maximum of 30 days, only on the specific product and outlet where the competitor price was observed.
